NovuBurn Ingredients

NovuBurn is marketed as a dietary supplement designed to complement a ketogenic or low-carbohydrate lifestyle. Its official ingredient page lists raspberry ketone, green tea extract, caffeine anhydrous, green coffee bean extract, and Garcinia cambogia in a 650 mg Keto Blend.

1

Raspberry Ketone

Raspberry ketone is a naturally occurring aromatic compound associated with the scent of red raspberries. It is used in some dietary supplements formulated for weight management. Although preliminary research has explored its potential effects on fat metabolism, strong evidence that raspberry ketone supplements cause meaningful weight loss in humans is lacking.
2

Green Tea Leaf Extract

Green tea leaf extract contains naturally occurring plant compounds called catechins, including epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG). These compounds have antioxidant properties and have been studied for their possible roles in metabolic health. The amount of green tea extract in NovuBurn should be confirmed from the product label.
3

Caffeine Anhydrous

Caffeine anhydrous is a concentrated, dehydrated form of caffeine commonly used in dietary supplements. Caffeine can temporarily increase alertness and reduce feelings of fatigue. However, excessive intake may cause jitteriness, anxiety, sleep disturbances, or a rapid heartbeat.
4

Green Coffee Bean Extract

Green coffee bean extract is derived from unroasted coffee beans and contains compounds such as chlorogenic acids. It is commonly included in metabolism-focused supplements. Research on its effectiveness for weight management remains limited, and it should not be considered a guaranteed weight-loss ingredient.
5

Garcinia Cambogia (Fruit)

Garcinia cambogia is a tropical fruit whose rind contains hydroxycitric acid (HCA). It is frequently used in weight-management products. However, clinical evidence for substantial weight-loss benefits is inconclusive, and some Garcinia cambogia supplements have been associated with liver-related safety concerns.
6

Cellulose (Vegetable Capsule)

The NovuBurn ingredient page lists cellulose as a vegetable capsule ingredient. Capsule materials are used to enclose the formula and make it convenient to consume. Check the current package label for the exact capsule material and any additional inactive ingredients.
